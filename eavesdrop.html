Eavesdrop

2026, Performance at the Cape Town Investec Art fair. LUX + KAHLIL

The theme for the 2026 Investec Cape Town Art Fair was "listen". We took this theme quite seriously in our conception of EAVESDROP, a performance piece running at the venue for the duration of the fair.

To eavesdrop is to listen in without permission, to overhear, or to snoop.

Our Routine
From the 20th - 22nd of February we did nearly 10 hours of listening. We did it in three shifts per day. 1 hour walking around, listening and writing what we heard into a notebook. 1 hour of transcribing and putting what we heard onto Instagram.

We wore formal suits with a large QR code sewn to the blazers back with a prompt that said "Have we been listening? Read what you've been saying." The QR code linked to Everard Read's instagram account.

The performance was routine-based and ultimately an experiment in listening. The fair is big and bustling with sound and thousands of utterances, interactions and exchanges. To really listen was challenging.
